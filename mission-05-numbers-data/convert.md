# Task 5.1 – Convert Numbers by Hand

## 1. Convert decimal 13 to binary

Divide by 2 repeatedly and note the remainders:

| Division | Remainder |
|---|---:|
| 13 ÷ 2 = 6 | 1 |
| 6 ÷ 2 = 3 | 0 |
| 3 ÷ 2 = 1 | 1 |
| 1 ÷ 2 = 0 | 1 |

Read the remainders from bottom to top.

**Answer: (13)₁₀ = (1101)₂**

## 2. Convert decimal 25 to binary

| Division | Remainder |
|---|---:|
| 25 ÷ 2 = 12 | 1 |
| 12 ÷ 2 = 6 | 0 |
| 6 ÷ 2 = 3 | 0 |
| 3 ÷ 2 = 1 | 1 |
| 1 ÷ 2 = 0 | 1 |

Read the remainders from bottom to top.

**Answer: (25)₁₀ = (11001)₂**

## 3. Convert binary 1010 to decimal

Multiply each digit by its corresponding power of 2:

= 1 × 2³ + 0 × 2² + 1 × 2¹ + 0 × 2⁰

= 8 + 0 + 2 + 0

**Answer: (1010)₂ = (10)₁₀**

## 4. Convert binary 1101 to decimal

= 1 × 2³ + 1 × 2² + 0 × 2¹ + 1 × 2⁰

= 8 + 4 + 0 + 1

**Answer: (1101)₂ = (13)₁₀**

## 5. Convert hexadecimal A to decimal

In hexadecimal, A represents 10.

**Answer: (A)₁₆ = (10)₁₀**

## 6. Convert hexadecimal 1F to decimal

In hexadecimal, F represents 15.

= 1 × 16¹ + 15 × 16⁰

= 16 + 15

**Answer: (1F)₁₆ = (31)₁₀**

## 7. Convert binary 1111 to hexadecimal

Group the binary digits into groups of 4:

1111

Convert the group to decimal:

= 1 × 8 + 1 × 4 + 1 × 2 + 1 × 1

= 15

In hexadecimal, 15 is represented by F.

**Answer: (1111)₂ = (F)₁₆**
